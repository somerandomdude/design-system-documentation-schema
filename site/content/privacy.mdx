# Privacy

This site doesn't use analytics, advertising, tracking cookies or third-party scripts. It doesn't store anything in your browser. Loading a page from this site doesn't contact any other service in any way.

Some pages link to other sites, such as GitHub, the W3C and json-schema.org. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and following a link takes you to them.

The site is hosted on Netlify. Netlify keeps standard server logs for each request, which can include your IP address, the page you asked for and your browser's user agent. Those logs are governed by Netlify's own privacy policy.
